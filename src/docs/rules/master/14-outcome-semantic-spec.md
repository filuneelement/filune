# FILUNE Outcome Semantic Spec

Status: assertion policy ready. Domain implementation follows the approved subset for each batch. Authority: A rev182, 25-7 rev16, and the source record in P4-D. B rev38 supplies publication constraints. The audit IDs identify evidence rows and do not constitute a runtime rule registry.

## Boundary

Outcome consumes the existing Canonical and scoped Judgment results as read-only input. An outcome record points to its source judgment, relation/participant, role and effect IDs where applicable. Each record carries the original scope and effective interval, evidence classification, and certainty. A domain label requires its own source-backed connection. Structural effects retain their original axis, direction, role state and function.

Reading Assembly handles chapter placement. Locale Renderer handles wording. Outcome records contain neither chapter IDs nor localized text. Customer-provided facts may resolve known context within an approved rule; they do not revise structural judgment.

## Outcome assertion policy

A confirmed structural condition and a verified source-supported real-life mapping establish the supported Outcome. Emit the life meaning at its evidenced strength. A structural label alone is insufficient when an approved mapping establishes what the condition means in life. Confirming earning capacity, a retention constraint or a period of pressure confirms that specific meaning in the FILUNE interpretation. The occurrence, deadline, amount and magnitude of a concrete event each require their own authority and evidence. Preserve source modality, scope, target, protection, competing paths and lifecycle.

Evaluate each result independently. Acquisition and retention can coexist with opposite directions. Duration and stability require their own evidence. Problem resolution, the means of resolution, who captures the benefit, and costs or side effects are separate assertions. For example, a verified harmful Killing path can be resolved through personal skill, or through another actor whose contribution changes the allocation of credit when separately supported. No overall score, good/bad reduction or cancellation of independently established results.

Use `deferred` per assertion when required engine evidence is absent, the source mapping is unresolved, or the source cannot resolve competing conditions on that same assertion. Preserve other confirmed results. For an evidenced earning path with unknown retention capacity, publish earning capacity at its source strength and defer retention. A genuine conditional assertion retains its trigger and conditional certainty. Lifecycle and evidence certainty remain independent.

Assembly carries the independent facts. The renderer expresses each approved meaning in concrete life terms while retaining its qualifications. Keep relationship and health publication constraints, known customer context, and product scope. For example, an evidenced earning capacity and an evidenced retention constraint can both appear in the same reading.

### Minimum contract for future mappings

Reuse `OutcomeFact.semanticType` as the result type with domain, scope, period, certainty, lifecycle and provenance. One fact per independently testable result, target and effective interval supports acquisition, retention, stability, duration, resolution, benefit capture, cost and side effect. Use a domain-specific payload for direction or attribution where required. Keep evidence axis (stem/branch) distinct from the life-result dimension. A shared path or mapping reference can link coexisting facts without making new universal fields mandatory. Preserve original IDs on chapter reuse.

An `OutcomeMappingRule` design record should specify the required Judgment fact references, required current state, life meaning and its modality, scope and exclusions, and primary/source authority provenance. Record counterexamples and unverified prerequisites. This is an audit contract for future implementation. [Mapping register](../../codex/p4-outcome-assertion-policy-correction-result.md)

### Health publication gate

Adequate, matched H2 evidence can express a traditional tendency for burden in the verified area. H3 adds an independently established time to pay attention. Preserve the result and interval in the semantic layer. Medical weakness, symptoms and unrestricted health difficulty copy remain outside the publication contract.

## Exclusions and deferred gates

- Work: position acquisition and retention, work difficulty, means of resolution, self/external credit and organizational influence require a follow-up source audit. Preserve case evidence as candidates for mapping. The [Work/Study audit](21-work-study-outcome-source-audit.md) documents candidate conditions and scope. The runtime producers remain deferred.
- Money: exact income, asset amounts, asset protection inferred from Resource, contract/business/spending/collaboration timing and selection of principles remain deferred pending specific support. Investment and borrowing instructions are excluded.
- Relationship: relationship style and guaranteed partner quality, marriage/divorce and compatibility are excluded from these projections. I-22 forms ③ and ④ remain deferred. Form ④ requires the approved favorable judgment of the same period and verified absence of damage to both the partner significator and SangShen. A missing check cannot satisfy this condition.
- Health: generalized timing and a separate relief-period semantic remain deferred. See the Health source contract for supported timing and prohibited outputs.
- Duplicates cannot count as independent evidence. This contract makes no top-three, best/worst month, question routing, or ranking decisions.

Use OP-L01/L02 for direct comparisons, inheritance and expiry. A higher-scope case cannot supply a missing monthly rule. Preserve separate stem and branch results per OP-L03 and I-14 evidence bookkeeping. No merged rating or conversion of opposite axes into a domain outcome has been approved. Lower certainty survives assembly and rendering.

Preserve reduced and conditional states; stopped and inactive do not contribute as active effects.
