# United States city data

`usCities.json` is a filtered local extract of the GeoNames `cities15000.zip` gazetteer, limited to United States populated places. The source includes coordinates and IANA timezone IDs; standard meridians are assigned by timezone for longitude correction.

Source: [GeoNames export dump](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip), retrieved 2026-10-04.
